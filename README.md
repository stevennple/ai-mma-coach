# AI Customer Support

An AI-powered MMA coach providing personalized training advice and workout plans based on user interactions.

## Usage

Simply click on the link to use the chatbot!

## Features

- **AI-powered MMA Coaching**: The AI MMA Coach acts as a chatbot that provides coaching for Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) enthusiasts. It uses advanced AI algorithms to simulate the coaching experience, offering guidance on techniques, strategies, and training methods.
- **Personalized Training Plans**: The AI MMA Coach creates personalized training plans based on the user's skill level, goals, and preferences. It adapts the training regimen to ensure continuous improvement and to cater to the specific needs of each user.
- **Real-time Feedback and Analysis**: The coach provides real-time feedback on the user's performance, analyzing their techniques and offering suggestions for improvement. This feature helps users to refine their skills and to receive immediate coaching tips during their training sessions.
- **Dynamic Coaching Insights**: Integrated with Gemini 1.5 Flash API, the chatbot generates dynamic coaching insights and adaptive training strategies to enhance user experience.
- **Optimized Performance**: Utilizing server-side rendering (SSR) in Next.js, the application reduces load times and improves user engagement.
